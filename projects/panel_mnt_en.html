🖥️ Panel MNT

{% include "switcher.html" %}

Panelist portal developed over 3 months for the French "Mutuelle Nationale Territoriale" (MNT), on a permanent contract at Soft Concept

📝 Presentation

This application allows the members of the mutual to participate in thematic surveys and to earn rewards in the form of gift vouchers in exchange.

🛠️ Technologies used

  • HTML, CSS, JavaScript
    • {% set extraTool = { slug: "panel-manager", name: "Panel Manager", desc: "survey management and panel monitoring" } %}
  • Internal tools: {% include "softconcept-tools.html" %}

🗂️ Application structure

👨‍💼 Back Office (Administrators)

  • 👥 Panelist management
  • 📅 Survey creation and scheduling
  • 📂 Data archiving and download
  • 📥 Import of results in PDF format

👤 Panelist Area (Users)

  • 🔑 Login to personal account
  • 📝 View and edit profile
  • 📊 Participate in surveys
  • 📜 Participation history
  • 📞 Support contact and account deletion

⚙️ Additional features

  • 📣 Notification sending
  • 🎁 Viewing points and rewards
  • ❓ FAQ to assist panelists

📈 Usage

The application is used by MNT members to contribute to surveys on health, prevention and mutual services. On the MNT side, Panel Manager is a management and analysis tool designed to gather feedback and optimize its services. Members are rewarded for participating in monthly surveys through digital gift vouchers provided by Glady.
