🖥️ OPALE SNITEM

{% include "switcher_en.html" %}

Audit platform developed over 1 year for SNITEM, on a permanent contract at Soft Concept, in collaboration with Nexialist

📝 Presentation

It's a management tool for overseeing the various stages leading to MDR (Medical Device Regulation) certification, on SNITEM (French National Association of Medical Technologies Industries) initiative.

🛠️ Technologies used

  • C#, HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Internal tools:
    • Net-Survey: questionnaire creation
    • Ethnos: survey data analysis
    • Web Reports: application development and deployment

🗂️ Application Structure

👨‍💼 Administrator Profile (SNITEM)

  • 🔍 Status Review - Diagnosis: consultation of indicators to understand the overall distribution of TD (Technical Documentation)
  • ✅ Decision: summary of decisions made within the company regarding the product portfolio
  • 🕹️ Monitoring: summary of management elements entered by manufacturers
  • 🔐 Access Management: management of rights for manufacturers and all users

👨‍🏭 Manufacturer Profile (Adherent)

  • 📦 Products: entry of data (market, financial, technical...)
  • 📋 QMS: management of quality management systems (ISO 13485)
  • 📁 TD: data entry and regulatory decision within the MDD/MDR transition
  • 🏢 Company Data: name, workforce, capital

⚙️ Additional Features

  • 📄 Data Import via Excel form
  • 📊 Data Visualization "micro/macro"
  • 📖 User Guide (documentation and deeper insights)

📈 Usage

  • 👉 Administrator / SNITEM: user management and access to the "macro dashboard"
  • 👉 Manufacturers: data entry and access to the "micro dashboard"
