🖥️ REF230 AFNOR

{% include "switcher_en.html" %}

Audit platform developed over 2 years for AFNOR, on a permanent contract at Soft Concept, in collaboration with Vitalis

📝 Presentation

This software is a management and monitoring tool for the REF230 certification, delivered by AFNOR, ensuring the quality of the services in the optical sector (ISO 9001).

🛠️ Technologies used

  • C#, HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Internal tools:
    • Net-Survey: questionnaire creation
    • Ethnos: survey data analysis
    • Web Reports: application development and deployment
    • Survey-Manager: survey management and mail launcher

🗂️ Application Structure

👨‍💼 Administrator / Customer Manager Profile (AFNOR)

  • 🏬 Store management (certified and new)
  • 📅 Request and scheduling of audits
  • 📑 Reading and editing of data sheets
  • 🔍 Viewing of reports
  • 📍 Certification decision

📑 Service Provider Profile (Vitalis / Présence)

  • 📅 Handling of scheduled audits
  • 📎 Entry of audit reports and summaries
  • ✅ Follow-up of completed audits

🏪 Brand Profile (e.g. Lissac)

  • 🔍 Consultation of certification files
  • 📂 Download of data sheets and related documents
  • 📊 Externalized DataViz

⚙️ Additional Features

  • 📥 Notifications and application status tracking by email
  • 📄 File import: K-bis, ADELI certificate...
  • 🎯 Custom statistics and interactive map
  • 🔍 Advanced filters: brand, postal code, workforce, legal status...

📈 Usage

  • 👉 AFNOR: global administration and user management
  • 👉 Customer Managers (TC): store management and audit monitoring
  • 👉 Service Providers: audit and report execution (non-compliance and proposition sheets)
  • 👉 Retail Brands: read-only store monitoring
