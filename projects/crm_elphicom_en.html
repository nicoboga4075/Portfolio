🖥️ CRM Elphicom

EN FR

CRM platform developed over 3 months during my end-of-studies internship at Soft Concept, a Lyon-based software publisher specializing in survey solutions (satisfaction, social barometers, HR surveys)

📝 Overview

Elphicom is a French company specializing in telephony and operator services. This CRM efficiently manages: clients and prospects, contracts and associated documents, call and intervention tracking.

🛠️ Technologies used

  • HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Internal tools:
    • Net-Survey: questionnaire creation
    • Ethnos: survey data analysis
    • Web Reports: application development and deployment

🗂️ Application structure

  • 📇 Clients and prospects: view, edit, delete (only for prospects)
  • 📃 Contracts: color-coded status (expired, to monitor, active), renew or removal
  • 📞 Calls: manage history with date, contact, reason, and follow-up actions
  • 📑 Document library: Word, Excel, PDF files, etc.

⚙️ Additional features

  • 📑 Detailed client records: contact, SIRET number, linked applications (Zendesk, Forest)
  • 📋 Field intervention reports via Mobi-Survey (offline mode)
  • 👥 Manage users (Administrator, Supervisor, Sales)

📈 Usage

The application is used daily by around twenty collaborators for client follow-up, and commercial and technical management.
