My career in few lines
I'm developer but what else ? Internships, contracts, projects, languages... A one-page overview to learn a bit more about my experiences.

My name is Nicolas, I'm 27 years old and I'm a software engineer with 4 years of professional experience (including internships). In 2021, I hold a diploma from ESILV, an engineering school specializing in digital technologies in Paris La Défense and a degree in informatics from UQAC in Canada. Throughout my career, I have completed 3 internships and held 2 permanent positions.

Internships

Galadrim

My first internship, lasting 2 months (from June to August 2019), was with the start-up Galadrim, a Paris-based development agency founded in 2017 by two polytechnicians : Arnaud ALBALAT and Jean ASHTON. Supervised by Clément DOURLENS (UI/UX Designer), my responsibilities included marketing (contacting leads, writing articles) and design. At the same time, I was trained in HTML, CSS and JavaScript by a confirmed developer : Gautier MULAK. At the time of my internship, Galadrim had an exclusively Parisian staff of 10 people. The company has grown considerably and now employs over 70 people in Paris, Nantes and Lyon. Focusing on Javascript technologies (React, Angular, Vue for the front-end and Node for the back-end), its activity is essentially web and mobile development using Agile method. To find out more, visit Welcome to the Jungle.

Cyberprotect

My second internship, lasting 6 months (from March to August 2020), was held in Bron (in the suburbs of Lyon) at Cyberprotect, an SME of about fifteen people specialized in cybersecurity founded in 2004 by Thierry LAMBERT. Supervised by Mikael MASSON (CTO) and Rémi ALLAIN (Senior Cybersecurity Engineer), I have independently developed a full stack security maintenance application, using Python for the back-end with the framework Flask, Angular for the front-end, and PostgreSQL for the database. I was able to release an alpha version and, as far as I know, it was subsequently migrated to another back-end technology. By the way, I wrote an article which describes the main concepts of this type of application, available here. Cyberprotect is a combination of software vendor, SOC and consulting and audit firm confirmed in managed security (MSSP). For more information, visit Welcome to the Jungle.

Soft Concept

My graduation internship, lasting 6 months (from July 2021 to January 2022) was with Soft Concept, an SME of about 10 people founded in 1986 in Lyon, where I was then hired as a permanent. Soft Concept is a Windows software vendor and hosting service provider (IIS) specialized in survey solutions (mobile, web and paper). As an intern, I have contributed to the evolution of Ethnos, a data analytics and processing software, integrating dynamic graphics with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Supervised by Gérard DANAGUEZIAN (former CEO) and Steeve GRÉGOIRE (Senior Software Engineer and current CEO), I also participated in several client projects that allowed me to better understand the company's software suite, natively implemented in Delphi and migrated specifically in C#.

Permanent contracts

Soft Concept

As a permanent, for nearly 2 and half years (from January 2022 to May 2024), I was part of the support team, serving as both technical assistant (30%: Level 1 & 2) and customer service developer (70%: C# .NET, HTML, CSS, Javascript, jQuery). Support (by phone, by email, using visio) included answering software questions (features, settings, licenses), training customers (demonstrations and remote assistance) and providing maintenance and consulting services. The projects were diverse and time-consuming : data retrieval applications (dataviz), showcase sites, panels and CRM. This variety of projects has been accompanied by an equally eclectic clientele, ranging from large accounts to SMEs, as well as research institutes, universities and associations. Each collaboration has enabled the exploration of various sectors of activity such as luxury, construction, transport, life science, auditing and catering. These projects (more than ten) required a medium or long-term investment, usually between 6 months and 2 years (compared with 1 to 3 months in internship) and enriched my ability to adapt to the specificities of the expectations of actors from different fields. As a stand-alone or in pairs (with Steeve, previously mentioned or Paul EUDELINE, Junior Software Engineer), I have also played an active role in project management (Agile or V Model) for planning, clarifying needs or presenting suitable solutions. Finally, I had the opportunity, along with the team, to participate in the E-Marketing Fair in March 2022, held in Paris. Indeed, the company is known for its involvement in a specialized journal in the field : Survey Magazine. To learn more, visit here.

Bassetti Group

My last experience as a permanent was with Bassetti Group in Lyon for 3 months (June to September 2024), a multinational software company with more than 500 employees, founded in 1992 by David BASSETTI and focused on industry and laboratory science solutions (LIMS). I joined the Lyon agency as a security engineer, with the mission of auditing and refactoring the back-end of a BI module of the software Teexma (the front-end being in Angular), applying security best practices to prevent vulnerabilities (SQL injections and XSS flaws notably). Supervised by Florian CULIÉ (Senior Security Engineer) and Antoine VILLEMAGNE (Junior Software Engineer based in Grenoble, the headquarters), I developed in C# .NET and used Powershell as well as Postman. To learn more about Bassetti, visit Welcome to the Jungle.
