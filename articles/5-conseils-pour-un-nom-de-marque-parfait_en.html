5 tips for the perfect brand name
Woken up with a revolutionary idea ? Want to become an entrepreneur ? There's no time to lose ! But first of all, you'll need a brand name.

This task may seem simple, but it can also present itself as a real headache for even the most creative entrepreneurs. To be effective, your brand name must be authentic, striking, eye-catching, easy to remember and pronounce, and above all perfectly reflect the image you want to give your company to your future customers or employees . The choice of naming is not trivial and therefore requires careful consideration. Indeed, the name of your brand will follow you throughout the public sphere and all the time. And above all, it will contribute significantly to the growth of your company's reputation. Also, it is essential to register your trademark.

I've come up with 5 key tips for you to consider when creating the perfect brand name.

Tip #1 : Create a universe around your brand

Before you define the name, you need to create a universe around your brand, even if that sounds abstract. This is why Jeff Bezos, the CEO of Amazon and the richest man in the world, said in an interview: " You can choose a brand name that refers to a specific product category. But in my opinion, this brand will not be as strong and will not have the same value as a brand evoking something more abstract ". For those who are interested, more tips on how to succeed can be found at Strategie.fr.

Now you have a sense of your identity, your history, your values and your actions. You also need to answer the following questions. Which market do you want to establish yourself in : regional, national or international ? What are your assets ? How do you manage competition ? So you'll have to work your imagination or that of your colleagues if you want to establish an ideal brand name.

Tip #2 : Focus on a single target

Did you do a good job of your neurons during the previous brainstorming ? Have you strayed too far from the main goal ? If that's the case, it's time to refocus on your brand universe. You must admit that through the later name of your brand, you will not be able to externalize all your thoughts. You'll have to make a selection, determine what is really fundamental and what actually makes you your best asset to distinguish yourself from your competitors .

To do this, start by identifying your target, the class of people you want to reach and your service or product will reach. Are they children, students, adults or seniors ? What are their nationalities, what languages do they speak ? Then how do you position yourself in relation to the market ? For example, if your products are discount-oriented, it's a good idea to eliminate names that inspire luxury. In addition, you need to be able to listen to your customers in order to find the key argument more easily. Positioning must always be consistent with the name. Customers have patterns and habits and it takes time to install a new positioning, so it’s important to make sure you find one that can sustain and serve the brand.

Tip #3 : Apply the right methods

Use concept names

Play puns and don't be afraid of the odd bit of humor ! For example, « To Brie or Not to Brie » for a cheese truck or « Nice to Meat You » for a butcher shop. Rely on implicit concepts or simply use a phrase or a forgotten word of morals.

Create an original name with smart keywords

Creative, you will be ! From the keywords of your concept, you will modify some syllables while keeping the basic structure, playing on the sounds. You can play on the combination of two names like : intuitive + software = Intuitive © or play on phonetics. Choosing a name that looks very simple can sometimes be very effective, as is the case for YouTube for example.

Create an acronym

You need to think of your brand logo and brand name at the same time. You can hire a graphic designer, freelancer or even a communications agency to create it. Keep in mind that a beautiful visual helps with memory.

Think of reputable references

Here you can choose names of famous people or places, historical references or even "invoke" a celebrity. However, you have to be careful because you then take over the authorization. Some brands have preferred to use a surname, usually that of the founder such as E.Leclerc, Franck Provost or Louis Vuitton.

Use foreign words

Foreign-sounding names are often more successful because they anticipate international market development. One can cite Facebook or Apple. However, you should think about learning about translation to avoid regretting your choice in the future. For example, choosing Kids Exchange for a children's business is a bit of a misnomer.

Comply with domain #1

Without 100% copying or legal problems, try to be inspired and bring out the idea gathered (benchmarking) with him while mixing it with yours to avoid counterfeiting, plagiarism.

Play on "numbers and letters"

It is an opportunity to grow and consult the Larousse or visit a specialized site such as Études Littéraires to give ideas of suffixes or prefixes. For example, Intuiware for intuitive computer software.

Tip #4 : Verify name compliance

Make sure your brand selection fits within the universe created in Tip #1 . You can do the test yourself by answering the following questions : What inspires you the name ? Is the target audience well targeted and will they be able to understand clearly ? Isn’t that too off-kilter ? Is this name easy to pronounce ? Does it also respond to possible developments in expanding activity in a larger market ? The ideal brand name should be no more than two words.

Tip #5 : File your new brand name

You're through it! You've finally picked up a couple of relevant names. The final step is to verify legal availability either in the INPI database (National Institute of Industrial Property) or on other specialized sites. Then, you will have to register the trademark (procedure) to be sure that the name of it will truly belong to you ! However, it is important not to overlook the fact that INPI can refuse your brand name if it misleads consumers. As a result, the proprietor cannot benefit from the "lapse of time" rule, which states that a trademark used within five years of its registration is protected.

A trademark is valid for an unlimited period of time as long as it is renewed every 10 years after its initial filing date. It is therefore a strategic investment for which the use of an intellectual property lawyer is recommended. It is still possible to redeem a brand name even if it is registered.

Use a brand name builder

Despite the tricks above, you may not be inspired to find the name or unfortunately the name may no longer be available. Don't get angry and stay zen ! Fortunately, Google has a panel of brand name generators, free and fast helping any future entrepreneur who is running out of ideas or perhaps lacking imagination. Each is unique : some are based on a root word (Nomatic), while others are based on keywords (Panabee). Others go even further by offering more than 100 declensions with a word or phrase (Lean Domain Search) or randomly based on a predefined syllable name (Nomino).

Kill two birds with one stone ! In fact, the majority of these generators often allow you to purchase directly the domain name linked to the future name of your brand. Moreover, there may be excesses linked to this purchase. In order to prevent competitors from obtaining it, some companies decide to purchase multiple brand names.
