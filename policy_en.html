Privacy policy

Updated on October 28, 2026

What data is collected?

The personal data used is that which the user explicitly provides in the contact form: sender's name, subject of the email and message content. This information is included in an email sent via the Gmail API and is not stored on the application server. When logging in via OAuth (manual authentication), your email address is retrieved from your Google account. No other personal or sensitive data is collected or used.

How is data used?

The information collected (notably your email address) is used exclusively for sending the message via the contact form. No other use is made of this data, except for technical purposes related to the sending of the email.

How is data stored and secured?

The email address is stored securely, in compliance with security standards, as an access token required to interact with the Gmail API, kept in the memory of the page only, never in your browser storage. This token is only available to authorized people or systems to send the message. The transfer of data between your device and the application is done by secure connection (HTTPS).

Cookies and audience measurement

With your consent only, the site uses Google Analytics to count its visits and see which pages are read. Nothing is sent to Google and no cookie is set before you click Accept in the banner. If you accept, Google Analytics sets the cookies _ga and _ga_<id>, which last 13 months at most. Your choice is kept for 6 months in your browser, then the banner asks again; you can change it at any time with the Manage cookies link at the bottom of every page, and refusing deletes these cookies.

Data sharing

The information collected (notably your email address) will not be shared, sold or used for commercial purposes.

Data retention and deletion

We will retain your personal information for the length of time needed to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy policy unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. The access token required for authentication through the Gmail API is securely retained and is automatically deleted in the event of an error or obsolete after prolonged inactivity. You can delete this data manually by deleting data from your browser. By default, the access token expires after 1 hour, according to Google's rules. Once the message is sent, only a copy of it (name, subject, message) is kept by Netlify Forms, the form service of the host, so that no message is lost. If you experience repeated errors sending the message, I suggest you refresh the page or open a new tab.

If you have any questions about your personal data, you can contact me via the form.
