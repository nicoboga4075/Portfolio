Privacy policy

Updated on December 4, 2024

What data is collected?

When logging in via OAuth, only the email address is retrieved from your account. No other personal or sensitive data is collected or used.

How is data used?

The collected email address is used exclusively to send the message through the form. No other use is made of this information.

How is data stored and secured?

The e-mail address is stored securely and in compliance with security standards. It is only available to authorized individuals or systems to send the message.

Data sharing

The information collected will not be shared, sold or used for commercial purposes. The e-mail address will only be used to manage communications related to the form.

If you have any questions about your personal data, you can contact me via the form.
